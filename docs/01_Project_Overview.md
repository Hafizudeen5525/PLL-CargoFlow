# CargoFlow: Project Overview

## What is CargoFlow?
CargoFlow is a lightweight commodity trading and risk management (CTRM) dashboard built for physical LNG and oil cargo operations. It helps commercial operators, traders, and risk analysts track deals throughout their lifecycle—from initial term sheet capture to physical discharge, market revaluation, and final invoice reconciliation.

## Why Build This?
Physical cargo trading involves a lot of moving pieces. Most desks start out using spreadsheets, but spreadsheets become fragile when dealing with:
- Formula-driven pricing tied to volatile indices (e.g., Henry Hub, Brent, TTF, JKM).
- Monthly forward curves that update daily and change unrealized book values.
- Differentiating between shipping-related costs (charter, boil-off, bunker fuel) and other operational fees (financing, inspection, hedging).
- Reconciling physical settlements against official TRMS (Trade & Risk Management System) data.

CargoFlow brings these tasks into a single browser interface without requiring heavy enterprise installations.

## Target Audience
- **Commercial Traders:** Check active positions, monitor unrealized P&L, and evaluate matching physical legs.
- **Cargo Operators:** Keep track of laycan windows, discharge dates, and route logistics.
- **Risk & Finance Analysts:** Inspect floating vs. fixed price exposure, verify forward curves, and flag cost discrepancies against accounting ledgers.
